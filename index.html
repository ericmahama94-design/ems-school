About Us

Who we are

Eric Memorial School is a community school dedicated to giving every child a strong academic foundation and a good character. Founded in memory of Eric, whose life was marked by generosity and learning, the school continues his legacy of service.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable education that develops confident, responsible and curious learners who contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading school known for academic excellence, discipline and innovation, especially in science and technology.

Our Values

  • Integrity and respect
  • Hard work and excellence
  • Faith and service
  • Curiosity and innovation

Our dedicated teachers deliver Mathematics, Science, English and Technology alongside sports, arts and moral education.
